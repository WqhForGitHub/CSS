font-family 字体族基础设置

font-family 指定字体族，多个字体用逗号分隔，浏览器按顺序尝试。最后应放通用字体族。
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Georgia, serif
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Helvetica, sans-serif
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Consolas, monospace
The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. system-ui
通用字体族（serif/sans-serif/monospace/cursive/fantasy）是必需的回退方案。
